# 🛒 Online Retail Business Intelligence & Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing online retail data using **Microsoft Power BI** to understand product performance, brand contributions, customer ordering patterns, geographical distribution, and pricing trends.

The dashboard transforms raw retail data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, DAX measures, and data analysis techniques. It helps identify popular products, evaluate brand performance, monitor order trends, and support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer order patterns across different products and brands.
- Identify top-performing products based on order frequency.
- Evaluate brand-wise order contributions and performance.
- Analyze geographical distribution of customer orders.
- Monitor monthly and yearly order trends.
- Understand the relationship between product prices and customer orders.
- Track overall order performance against business targets.
- Develop interactive dashboards to support business decision-making.

## 📂 Dataset Information

**Dataset Name:** Online Retail Dataset

**Table Name:** `online_retail_real_world`

**Dataset Size:** 3,000 records

### Dataset Columns

| Column Name | Description |
|---|---|
| OrderID | Unique order identification number |
| CustomerID | Customer identification number |
| ProductName | Name of the purchased product |
| Brand | Brand associated with the product |
| Raw_Weight | Product weight or package size |
| Country | Country information associated with the order |
| OrderDate | Date of the order |
| UnitPrice | Price per unit of the product |

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Product Order Performance:** Product order analysis highlights differences in customer demand across products, helping identify popular products and those receiving comparatively fewer orders.

2. **Brand-Wise Performance:** Brand performance varies based on customer orders, allowing businesses to identify leading brands and understand their contribution to overall order distribution.

3. **Geographical Distribution:** Geographical analysis reveals how customer orders are distributed across different countries, helping identify important markets and potential opportunities for business expansion.

4. **Monthly Order Trends:** Monthly order analysis highlights fluctuations in customer ordering patterns over time, helping identify periods of higher and lower order activity.

5. **Product Price Analysis:** Comparing average product prices with order counts helps understand the relationship between product pricing and customer demand.

6. **Customer and Order Performance:** Comparing yearly unique customers with total orders provides insights into customer activity and changes in the customer base over time.

7. **Order Target Achievement:** Comparing actual customer orders against the predefined order target helps evaluate overall business performance.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Identify top-performing and low-performing products to optimize product selection and inventory planning.
- Analyze brand-wise order distribution to strengthen relationships with popular brands.
- Focus on countries with higher customer order activity to explore geographical business opportunities.
- Monitor monthly order trends to plan suitable marketing campaigns.
- Evaluate product pricing and order patterns to develop effective pricing strategies.
- Encourage repeat purchases through improved customer engagement.
- Monitor order target achievement to evaluate business performance.
- Use interactive Power BI dashboards to support data-driven business decisions.
